## How to answer as the user

Answer each question the workflow asks you, one at a time, as the user.

Agree with what it proposes. When it proposes a name, a description, a role or
a set of tags, accept the proposal rather than substituting your own wording,
unless the proposal is plainly wrong about the repository. You are a user who
is happy to be given good suggestions.

Do **not** ask it to fill everything in for you in this run: answer each
question as it arrives. With its first proposal it offers to fill in the rest
itself. That offer is the one proposal to turn down: decline it, and go
through the remaining details one at a time.
