# Marketing Ideation: Audience

Use the brief in `.spektacular/working-context.md` and identify the people this campaign must reach.

Produce:

- primary audience and one secondary audience;
- their current belief or pain point;
- the change in belief or action this campaign should cause;
- channels where that audience already pays attention.
